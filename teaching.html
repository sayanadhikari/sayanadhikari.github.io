Teaching

Lecture materials from the plasma physics course I taught at the University of Oslo. Earlier, I taught computational physics to doctoral students at the Centre of Plasma Physics in India.

FYS4620/9620, An Introduction to Plasma Physics

Postgraduate course, Department of Physics, University of Oslo. Spring 2022.

The course covers the basic principles of plasmas, waves in plasma, and the stability of equilibrium solutions, with examples from the Earth's ionosphere and magnetosphere. Students learn to formulate the fluid equations for plasma from MHD to kinetic scales, derive dispersion relations, and build a simplified model of the magnetosphere.

Recommended background: FYS2160 Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics and FYS2140 Quantum Physics.
