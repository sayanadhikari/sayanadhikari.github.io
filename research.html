Space missions I contributed to

Emirates Lunar Mission

The Rashid rover carried a Langmuir probe from the University of Oslo to study the plasma and dust environment at the lunar surface. I modelled the probe's response to the photoelectron sheath.

Space Science Reviews paper, 2025 Nature news Going to the Moon

NanoMagSat

A nanosatellite constellation for mapping the geomagnetic field and ionospheric environment. I worked on simulations of the multi-needle Langmuir probe design.

EGU 2021 presentation

Daedalus

A proposed low-flying spacecraft to explore the lower thermosphere and ionosphere, where collisions between charged and neutral particles change how probes respond.

Mission site
